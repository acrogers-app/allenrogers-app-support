Voto: Live Polls — Support

Thank you for using Voto! Here are answers to common questions:

How do I create a poll?

Tap the + button on the home screen, enter your question and options, then tap Create.

How do I share a poll?

Open any poll and tap the Share button. Anyone can vote using the link — no app required.

Can I see results in real time?

Yes! Results update live as votes come in. Open your poll to watch votes appear instantly.

How do I close a poll?

Open the poll, tap the menu icon, and select Close Poll. No new votes can be submitted after closing.

How do I delete a poll?

Swipe left on any poll in your list and tap Delete. This permanently removes the poll and all its votes.

I voted but my vote didn't count — what happened?

Make sure you have an active internet connection. If the problem persists, email us below.
